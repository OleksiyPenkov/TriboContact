Hardness-to-Yield Estimation (Tabor Relation)

When only indentation hardness H is known for a coating or substrate, clicking Estimate σy = H / 2.8 applies the empirical Tabor constraint relation:

σY ≈ HC,   default constraint factor C = 2.8 (elastic-plastic metals & hard coatings ≈ 2.8–3.0)
