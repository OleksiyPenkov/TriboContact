Automated CalculiX Axisymmetric FEM (Engine 2)

Graded conforming mesh generation, 2° axisymmetric wedge scaling, penalty contact, secant load matching, and J2 plasticity

1. Why Elastic-Plastic FEM Matters for Coated Tribotests

Under realistic laboratory loads, ductile metallic or polymeric substrates yield beneath thin ceramic coatings. Once plastic flow begins, classical and layered linear-elastic models significantly overpredict peak pressure pmax and underpredict contact radius a: plastic flow flattens the substrate support, spreads load over a larger area, lets hard coatings flex downward (increasing tensile/compressive bending stress σrr inside the film), and leaves a permanent impression depth hr > 0 upon unloading.

2. Parametric Graded Conforming Mesh

TriboContact generates structured 4-node axisymmetric quadrilateral meshes directly in Delphi (TriboContact.FemMesh.pas) without any external mesher dependency:

  • Domain Extents — Automatically sized from Engine 1 contact radius aest and yield ratio: aeff = aest max(1, 0.58 √ry) when plasticity is active; far-field boundaries satisfy Rdom ≥ max(20 aeff, 20 htotal) and Hsub ≥ max(20 aeff, 20 htotal).
  • Density Presets — Radial elements across aeff: 15 (coarse), 24 (standard), 36 (fine), with geometric grading toward the far field and symmetric interface refinement through every coating layer.
  • Deformable / Rigid Ball — Generates a graded quarter-sphere ball mesh (0.75 Δrfine rigid, 1.0 Δrfine deformable), enforcing positive quad Jacobians, counter-clockwise orientation, zero duplicate nodes, and exact interface node sharing.

3. CalculiX Axisymmetric Deck & Numerical Formulation

  • 2° Wedge Convention — CalculiX treats 2D axisymmetric elements as a 2° sector. Therefore total 360° normal force from nodal vertical reactions is:
FN,360° = 180 × | ΣBALL_TOP RF2 |
(1)
  • Elements & Symmetry Axis Safeguard — Uses linear 4-node CAX4 quads (CAX4I maps cleanly to CAX4 on CalculiX 2.23, avoiding incompatible-mode element limitations on SURFACE TO SURFACE). Axis nodes at exact r = 0 are emitted as r = 10−3 Δrmin with Ux = 0 so CalculiX does not drop the innermost contact segment at r = 0.
  • Penalty Contact & Geometric Nonlinearity — Uses *CONTACT PAIR, TYPE=SURFACE TO SURFACE (slave S3 top face, master S1 ball arc), *STEP, NLGEOM, and auto-scaled linear penalty stiffness Kp capped against sphere facet sagitta s = Δr2 / (8R).
  • Displacement Control & Secant Load Matching — Ramps prescribed downward displacement −δ on BALL_TOP (never force-controlled from F = 0) and converges |FN,360° − Ftarget| / Ftarget ≤ 1% in at most 3 secant brackets.
  • Pressure-Integral Force Balance — Every converged solve integrates nodal contact pressure over deformed radial coordinates rdef = R + Ur and verifies force balance within < 3%.

4. J2 Plasticity, Hardening, and Unloading

When yield stress σY is defined for a layer or substrate, Engine 2 emits a von Mises J2 isotropic hardening card (*PLASTIC). Tabular true-stress vs. plastic-strain curves are written directly; bilinear inputs using tangent modulus Et are converted to plastic hardening modulus H':

H' = E · EtE − Et,   σ(0) = σY,   σ(0.1) = σY + 0.1 H'
(2)

For deep elastic-plastic indentation of an elastic-perfectly-plastic half-space (small Et), mean contact pressure approaches the classic Tabor constraint plateau pmean ≈ 2.8–3.0 σY, accompanied by positive equivalent plastic strain (PEEQ > 0) and permanent residual depth hr > 0 when the optional unloading step is enabled.

Per-Region Stress Recovery

CalculiX nodal averages in .frd smear stresses across shared interface nodes between stiff and compliant materials. Engine 2 builds per-region element connectivity graphs and extrapolates/projects stresses within each material domain independently so zk− and zk+ interface jumps remain sharp and uncorrupted.

Axisymmetric Limitation
Engine 2 models axisymmetric normal indentation and unloading (μ = 0 in the 2D axisymmetric FEM mesh; sliding friction tangential tractions are modeled by Engine 1's 3D harmonic elastic solver).
